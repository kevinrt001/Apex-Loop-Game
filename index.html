HOW TO PLAY

Driving

WASD
or Arrow Keys — accelerate, steer, and brake/reverse.
Space
Hold for a boost — extra speed and acceleration while your boost meter lasts. It recharges when you let go.
R
Respawn on the racing line at the point you went off. Costs you two seconds.

Camera & Menus

C
Cycle camera: Follow → Orbit (click-drag to look around) → Cockpit.
EscP
Pause the race — resume, restart, or return to the menu.

Opponents

Pick how many AI cars race with you from the menu — up to 15. They drive the same cars, on the same physics, and can be bumped. Watch the standings panel top-left to see your position.

On-screen info

Speed & Altitude — bottom-left, your current speed and hover height.
Boost meter — drains while boosting, refills when you're not.
Lap panel — top-right, current lap, current/best lap time.
Off track — dirt gives you less grip, less drive and a lower ceiling.
Mini-map — bottom-right. A fixed, north-up view of the whole circuit; the arrow shows your position and heading. Cyan = tunnels, green = ramps, coloured dots = rivals.

Watch out for

Pylons sit just inside the racing line — clipping one scrubs your speed. Trees, rocks and buildings crowd the verges and do not move.

Ramps

Orange-lit ramps sit on the racing line. Hit one at speed and you'll launch into the air.

Tunnels

Every circuit runs through three tunnels. The walls are solid — clip one at speed and you'll scrape along it.
